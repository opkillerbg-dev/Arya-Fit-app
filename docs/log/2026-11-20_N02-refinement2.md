## Changed
1. **Status bar overlap.**
   - Cause (code reading): with edge-to-edge the status bar is transparent. The old layout was `verticalScroll` > `safeDrawingPadding` > `padding`, so the safe-area padding was part of the scrolling content. At the top the content sat below the bar, but once scrolled the text slid up underneath the clock and icons.
   - Fix: the scroll content keeps `safeDrawingPadding()` inside the scroll (first screen starts below the bar, last item clears the navigation bar, no extra padding). A fixed `Spacer` with `windowInsetsTopHeight(WindowInsets.statusBars)` and the ivory background is drawn above the scroll in a `Box`. It is invisible at the top and hides scrolled text beneath the bar. Edge-to-edge is kept; no arbitrary dp values.
   - Icons stay dark: `MainActivity` and `themes.xml` untouched from refinement 1.
2. **Display typography (`Type.kt`, no new font, no scaling).** Big Shoulders caps are already tall (cap height 0.80 em, measured from the font file), so: heroStat 64/68 to 72/66; displayLarge 52/56 to 60/55; displayMedium 40/44 to 44/40 (tracking 0.3); screenTitle 34/38 to 36/33 (tracking 0.3). Line height about 0.92 em, stacked lines about 0.12 em apart. Rule: uppercase, 1 to 3 words; sentences stay in Inter. Instrument Serif and Inter styles unchanged.
3. **Specimen.** New "Display" section: "Heavy day" accent over "LEG DAY"; stacked "LOWER / BODY"; "WEEK 3 · PUSH"; "GOOD MORNING"; rule caption. Hierarchy notes show the new sizes. Everything else kept.
