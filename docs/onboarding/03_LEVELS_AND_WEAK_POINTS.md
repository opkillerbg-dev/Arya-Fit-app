# 03 Levels and weak points

**Level scale 0-5 per pattern:** 0 Starter (needs an easier version), 1 Beginner, 2 Developing, 3 Solid, 4 Advanced, 5 Strong (progress to harder variations or weight, not more reps).

| Level | Push-ups (max) | Pull-ups (max) | Squats (BW, max) | Plank | Run non-stop |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | 0-2 | cannot hang / hang only | - (needs chair squat) | - | cannot |
| 1 | 3-9 | 1-2 | under 15 | under 20 s | about 1 km |
| 2 | 10-19 | 3-5 | 15-29 | 20-44 s | about 3 km |
| 3 | 20-34 | 6-9 | 30-49 | 45-89 s | about 5 km |
| 4 | 35-49 | 10-14 | 50-99 | 90-179 s | about 10 km |
| 5 | 50+ | 15+ | 100+ (then single-leg) | 3 min+ | 15 km+ |

`legs` = squat level (lower by 1 if he reports knee pain). `core` = plank level. `run` = running level. If a benchmark is skipped: from experience (beg 1, int 2, adv 3).
**Gym lifts** (optional, approximate "strength standards", ratio of lift to body weight; mark as estimates): squat 0.5 / 0.75 / 1.0 / 1.5 / 2.0, bench 0.4 / 0.6 / 0.9 / 1.1 / 1.4, deadlift 0.75 / 1.0 / 1.5 / 1.9 / 2.4, overhead press 0.3 / 0.45 / 0.6 / 0.8 / 1.0 for levels 1-5. Level = highest row reached; a lift can raise a bodyweight level by at most 1.

**Weak point rule.** `top = max(push, pull, legs, core)`. A pattern is weak if `level <= top - 2`, or the person picked it in "want to improve". Worked example: push 5, pull 2, legs 3, core 3 -> pull is weak (gap 3). At most 2 weak patterns (largest gaps first); `run` is weak only if the goal is Run further.

**Ramp-in.** Week 1 starts one level lower than the benchmark and is a test week (stop 2 reps before failure, log reps). Week 2 uses the logged reps to confirm or adjust.

**Prescription by level (home).** L0-1: 2-3 x 6-10 of an easier variation (incline, band-assisted, negatives, rows). L2-3: 3 x 8-12. L4-5: 3-4 x 5-10 of a harder variation (decline, archer, weighted, single-leg). Rule: choose the ladder step where his max is about 12-20 reps, work sets end 1-2 reps from failure. Add a rep or a set before moving up a step (double progression).

**Weak-point handling.** More weekly sets (about +40%, at most +4) taken from the strongest pattern, trained at least 3 days a week when days >= 3, placed FIRST in the session, easiest-to-progress ladder step, optional short "practice sets" on rest days (e.g. 2 x 3 scapular pull-ups). Retest in 4 weeks; if the gap closes to 1 the priority is removed.

**Safety caps.** Health "check" or "gentle": max 2 sets per exercise, no max-effort tests, longer rest. Under 18: no 1-rep or weighted max tests, volume cap 12 sets per session. Protect-areas remove exercises with that tag in `av` (see 05). Never promise results or give medical advice.
